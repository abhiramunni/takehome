Five things to check before you sign

1
Fixed vs variable

A big variable slice looks great in the headline but may arrive once a year — or not at all. Compare fixed monthly pay, not CTC.

2
Basic pay is not a detail

Higher Basic means higher EPF and gratuity — less in hand now, more saved. Lower Basic means more cash today.

3
Employer PF & gratuity are not salary

They sit inside your CTC but never reach your bank account. Roughly 17% of Basic never becomes take-home.

4
The ₹12.75 lakh sweet spot

Under the new regime, a salary up to ₹12.75 lakh pays zero income tax. Just above it, marginal relief softens the first step up.

5
Old regime only wins with big deductions

It usually needs serious HRA + 80C + 80D + home-loan interest to beat the new regime. Check the comparison above before choosing.

Negotiate on in-hand, not on CTC.

Two offers with the same CTC can differ by ₹10,000 a month once tax, PF and variable pay are counted. Run both through the vault above — then ask for the one that pays more, not the one that sounds bigger.
